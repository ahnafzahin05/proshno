# Proshno — visual identity brief

Use this brief when creating or adapting Proshno brand materials. The colors and type choices below reflect the current website. The supplied logo assets in `assets/` are now integrated into the site and should remain consistent with the calm, study-first character described here.

## What Proshno is

Proshno is a public university admission question bank for English-version students in Bangladesh. It brings past questions from universities such as BUET, KUET, RUET, CUET, IUT, MIST, BUTEX, and Dhaka University into one organized study space.

The name **Proshno** is the English transliteration of the Bangla word **প্রশ্ন**, meaning “question.” The product should feel like a dependable study companion: focused, thoughtful, welcoming, and credible. Students may spend hours reading and practicing here, so the identity should feel easy on the eyes and mentally quiet while still being recognizable and distinctive.

## Brand character

- **Calm and focused:** visual clarity over decoration; no loud gradients or neon colors.
- **Scholarly and approachable:** a classic editorial touch balanced by a friendly modern interface.
- **Trustworthy and grounded:** serious enough for exam preparation, never institutional or intimidating.
- **Minimal and lasting:** simple forms that remain legible in a browser tab, on a phone, and in print.

Avoid generic education imagery such as graduation caps, stacks of books, lightbulbs, trophies, laurel wreaths, and default academic crests. Avoid AI-generated gloss, 3D effects, excessive detail, and symbols that could be mistaken for a university’s official seal. Do not use Bangladesh’s flag as the main color formula; local context can be subtle.

## Color palette

Use the deep green as the core brand color. The muted gold is a small supporting accent, not a second primary color. Build light and dark logo variants so the mark remains clear on both website themes.

| Page background | `#EEF0EB` | `#121916` | Soft sage-tinted neutral, not stark white or pure black |

The logo should work as a single-color mark in the main green, white, and dark ink. The full-color version may use the brand green with a restrained gold detail. Keep sufficient contrast on both `#F7F8F4` and `#1B2420` surfaces.

## Typography

The website pairs an editorial serif with a warm, modern sans serif:

- **Source Serif 4** — display headings, the Proshno wordmark direction, and long question text. It adds a considered, bookish quality without making the interface feel old-fashioned.
- **DM Sans** — interface labels, supporting copy, navigation, buttons, and controls. It is friendly and clear at small sizes, and softens the serif’s formality.
- **Hind Siliguri** — Bengali fallback for Bangla text. Keep it available even though the main audience and question content are English-version students.

The logo should complement this pairing. A custom, readable serif wordmark is a good starting direction; a clean sans-serif wordmark is also acceptable if the symbol carries the editorial character. Avoid ornate scripts, overly geometric techno fonts, and thin strokes that disappear at small sizes.

## Logo direction

Create a distinctive, compact symbol that connects **questions, learning, and forward progress** without relying on a literal school icon. A promising direction is a refined monogram built around the **P** in Proshno, with a subtle question-mark, open-page, or dialogue cue integrated into its negative space. Keep the metaphor simple enough to recognize at favicon size.

The symbol should feel balanced and quietly confident, with soft corners or carefully eased geometry rather than sharp corporate aggression. Prefer a solid, legible silhouette. Do not make it look like a generic chat app, search product, government seal, or university crest.

## Deliverables to request

1. A standalone symbol for favicon, app icon, and compact sidebar use.
2. A horizontal lockup with the symbol and the exact Latin name **Proshno**.
3. A light-background and dark-background version.
4. A one-color version that remains recognizable without gradients or shadows.
5. A square icon crop with safe padding and no text.

The design should remain recognizable at 16–24 px. Keep essential shapes thick and open; avoid tiny cutouts or fine lines. Provide a transparent-background version. If a raster output is supplied, use a large square canvas; a clean vector-style result is preferred so it can be redrawn or converted to SVG.
